Your documents stay with you.

Privacy policy · Updated September 14, 2026 · Perksh

Photos, documents and notes

Remedy Folder stores your item details, original photos and PDFs, preparation lists, contact records and outcomes on your device. Label-text recognition happens on the device. We do not upload this evidence to our servers. Your operating system may include app data in backups according to your Apple settings.

Official recall searches

When you search, your entered product words are sent to the public U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recall service. That service receives ordinary network request information such as your IP address. Your photos and supporting documents are not sent with a search. Saved notices include their source and retrieval date.

Purchases

Apple and RevenueCat process the optional lifetime purchase and restore requests. RevenueCat uses an anonymous customer identifier and purchase history to provide access, validate purchases, and supply purchase analytics in the developer dashboard. We do not supply a name, email, advertising identifier or your product evidence to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

You choose when to create an evidence pack and preview it before sharing. The system share sheet sends the PDF and original attachments only to the destination you select. That destination and its recipients control the copies you share. Documents may contain personal information you entered, so review the preview and attachments first.

Deletion

Delete a folder using its actions menu to remove its locally saved records and attachments. Previously exported copies, shared copies, and device backups are managed separately. Removing the app removes its local data; it does not cancel or refund an Apple purchase.

No tracking or app account

The app has no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, location collection, address-book access or app account registration. Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo. The photo picker lets you choose specific images without granting unrestricted access to your library.

Questions

Use the contact information on the support page. Please describe the issue without sending product evidence or identity documents unless you choose to share information necessary for troubleshooting.

2026年9月14日更新 · Perksh

写真・書類・記録

製品情報、元の写真・PDF、準備リスト、連絡記録と結果は端末に保存します。ラベルの文字認識も端末内で行い、開発者のサーバーにアップロードしません。Appleの設定に応じて端末のバックアップに含まれる場合があります。

公式リコール検索

入力した英語の製品名とIPアドレスなどの通常の通信情報を米国CPSCの公開サービスへ送信します。添付写真や書類は送信しません。公式情報は情報源と取得日を付けて保存します。

購入と復元

AppleとRevenueCatが買い切り購入と復元を処理します。RevenueCatは匿名の顧客IDと購入履歴を権限の確認と購入分析に使用します。名前・メール・広告ID・製品の資料はRevenueCatに渡しません。

共有と削除

資料をプレビューしてから、共有先を自分で選びます。選んだ宛先にPDFと元ファイルを共有します。共有済みのコピーは受信者が管理します。フォルダの操作メニューから削除すると端末内の資料を削除します。共有済みのコピーとバックアップは別に管理されます。アプリを削除してもAppleでの購入は返金されません。

追跡と権限

広告SDK、アプリ間の追跡、位置情報収集、連絡先へのアクセス、アプリ用アカウント登録はありません。カメラは写真を撮るときのみ許可を求めます。写真選択は選んだ画像だけを利用します。

お問い合わせはサポートへ。不要な製品の証拠書類や本人確認書類は送らないでください。
