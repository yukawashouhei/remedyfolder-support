REMEDY FOLDER

A recall notice.
A clear next step.

Keep your owned product's label, official instructions, requested documents and the company's response in one place.

Start a folder

Choose a name you recognize. Add a brand, model or serial text and any purchase details you want to keep. In Evidence, add a photo of the product label. Read label text can recognize it on your iPhone; review and edit every character before saving.

Find the official details

Use short product words, such as “high chair” or “power bank,” to search the public CPSC recall service. Compare the exact description, dates and model ranges with your item. Saving a notice does not establish that it applies. Record your matching decision yourself, and contact the company if anything is unclear.

Prepare an evidence pack

Add your own preparation items based on the company's current instructions. Attach original images or PDFs up to 25 MB each. Preview evidence pack creates a document with your notes, the saved notice, your contact record and an attachment index. The share sheet includes both the PDF and unchanged original files. You choose the destination.

Work without a connection

Your saved folders, files and notices can be edited and exported offline. New CPSC searches and App Store purchases require a connection. A network error is not a finding that no recall exists.

Purchases and restore

Your first folder, official information and complete exports are free. The optional lifetime Pro purchase enables multiple folders and household labels. Open About & settings, then Pro, then Restore purchases to restore a purchase made with the same Apple Account. Saved folders remain readable and exportable.

Keep or delete your records

Export a copy before deleting a folder if you want to keep it. Delete folder removes its local documents. Shared copies and device backups are managed separately.

About the source

Remedy Folder is independent and is not endorsed by CPSC. The company determines recall eligibility and the remedy. The app does not submit claims, send messages, issue refunds, diagnose problems, or certify product safety. Follow the current instructions in the official CPSC notice.
